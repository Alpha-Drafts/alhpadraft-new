## 2. Product Overview

DocAuditor is a **pre-submission verification tool**. It sits between "I'm done writing" and "I'm ready to submit." It answers three critical questions for anyone who submits written work:

1. **Is my work original?** — AI Originality Check
2. **Are there any source matches?** — Plagiarism Source Check
3. **Did I cover every requirement?** — Alignment Brief Check

DocAuditor does NOT rewrite, edit, or generate content. It flags problems and tells the user exactly what to fix. The user retains full control over their words.

### One-Sentence Description

> DocAuditor checks your documents for AI flags, source matches, and missing requirements before you submit — giving you sentence-level detail on exactly what to fix.

### Product Category

Pre-submission document verification platform.

## 3. The Problem We Solve

### The Landscape

Every day, millions of documents are submitted for evaluation — student essays, research papers, grant proposals, RFP responses, client reports. The people reviewing these documents are increasingly scrutinizing them for:

- **AI-generated content** — Universities, publishers, and clients are actively detecting and penalizing AI-assisted writing
- **Plagiarism and unattributed sources** — Academic and professional integrity standards are higher than ever
- **Missing requirements** — Incomplete submissions waste time, lose marks, and cost contracts

### The Gap

Most writers have no reliable way to check their own work against all three of these criteria before submitting. Existing tools address one problem at a time (a plagiarism checker here, a separate AI detector there) but none combine all three checks into a single, actionable report.

### What Happens Without DocAuditor

- Students lose marks or face academic integrity investigations for unintentional AI flags or missed citations
- Researchers get manuscripts returned or rejected for formatting gaps or source issues
- Professionals lose contracts because proposals don't address every RFP requirement
- Grant writers miss funding criteria they didn't realize they overlooked

### What Happens With DocAuditor

- Writers see every issue — AI flags, source matches, and missing requirements — in one color-coded report
- They fix problems in the same editor where they see them
- They recheck to confirm the fix worked
- They submit with full confidence

After editing flagged sections, users can rerun checks to verify their fixes resolved the issues.

## 6. The Three Checks — In Detail

### 6.1 AI Originality Check

**What it does:** Scans each sentence in the document for patterns commonly found in AI-generated text. Returns a percentage score and highlights every sentence that triggers a flag.

**How it works (simplified for marketing):** The check analyzes writing patterns — sentence structure, word choice, predictability — against known AI writing signatures. It does not simply compare against a database; it evaluates the statistical fingerprint of the writing itself.

**What the user sees:**

- An overall AI originality percentage (e.g., "12% of content flagged as potentially AI-generated")
- Indigo-highlighted sentences in the document where AI patterns were detected
- Signal strength per sentence (how strong the AI pattern match is)

**Why it matters:**

- Universities are actively running AI detection on student submissions
- Publishers and journals are flagging AI-assisted manuscripts
- Employers and clients may reject work they suspect is AI-generated
- DocAuditor lets users catch these flags first and rewrite before submission

**Important nuance for marketing:** DocAuditor flags patterns that _match AI writing characteristics_. It does not make a definitive determination that content was or was not written by AI. Results should be reviewed by the user. This is an important distinction — we flag, we don't accuse.

**Credit rate:** 0.2 credits per word

---

### 6.2 Plagiarism Source Check

**What it does:** Scans the user's text against published sources — journal articles, web pages, academic databases — and surfaces any matches with direct links to the original source.

**How it works (simplified for marketing):** The check breaks the document into segments and compares them against a broad index of published content. When a match or close paraphrase is found, it returns the source URL, the matching text, and the overlap location in the document.

**What the user sees:**

- A count of source matches found
- Red/rose-highlighted sentences in the document where matches were detected
- A list of matched sources with direct links and journal/publication details
- Actionable suggestions (e.g., "This sentence closely matches [source]. Add a citation or rephrase.")

**Why it matters:**

- Unintentional plagiarism is common — writers absorb phrasing from sources they've read
- Missing citations can result in academic penalties, journal rejection, or legal issues
- DocAuditor catches these overlaps before a reviewer does, giving the user time to add proper attribution or rephrase

**Credit rate:** 0.35 credits per word

---

### 6.3 Alignment Brief Check

**What it does:** Compares the submitted document against the user's assignment instructions, rubric, RFP requirements, or project brief. Returns a checklist showing which requirements have been met and which are missing.

**How it works (simplified for marketing):** The user uploads their document AND pastes their assignment instructions or requirements. The check extracts every requirement from the brief, then evaluates the document against each one. It produces a requirement-by-requirement checklist with status (met/not met) and gap analysis.

**What the user sees:**

- A count of missing requirements
- Amber/yellow-highlighted sections where requirements are not addressed
- A checklist showing every requirement extracted from the brief: met vs. still needs work
- Specific guidance on what's missing (e.g., "The brief asks for a cost-benefit analysis — this was not found in your document.")

**Why it matters:**

- This is DocAuditor's most unique feature — no mainstream competitor offers this
- Students frequently lose marks for "not addressing the question" even when their writing is otherwise strong
- Grant writers and proposal teams miss funding criteria they didn't realize were required
- This check turns a vague brief into a concrete checklist, then verifies the document against it

**This is our strongest differentiator.** Emphasize this in all marketing.

**Credit rate:** 0.3 credits per word

## 7. The Editor Experience

DocAuditor includes a full integrated editor where users can write, check, and revise without leaving the platform.

### Editor Layout

| Panel                    | Content                                                                       |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Left Panel**           | Full document editor with rich text formatting and color-coded highlights     |
| **Right Panel (Top)**    | Verification summary — AI %, source matches count, missing requirements count |
| **Right Panel (Bottom)** | Prioritized issue feed — clickable list of problems to fix                    |

### Editor Capabilities

- **Rich text formatting:** Bold, italic, headings (H1-H3), code blocks, bullet and numbered lists, blockquotes
- **Undo/Redo** functionality
- **Word and character count** displayed in real-time
- **Reading time** estimation
- **Auto-save** — work is saved automatically as the user types
- **5 snapshot versions** per document for recovery/comparison
- **Format removal** tools for cleaning up pasted text

### How Highlights Work

When a check is complete, flagged sentences are highlighted directly in the editor text:

- **Indigo highlights** = AI Originality flags
- **Red/Rose highlights** = Plagiarism/source match flags
- **Amber/Yellow highlights** = Missing requirement flags

Users click any highlighted sentence to see the specific issue details and suggested fix in the right panel. Users click any issue in the right panel to jump to the highlighted sentence in the editor.

### Edit-Recheck Cycle

1. User sees a flagged sentence
2. User rewrites the sentence in the editor
3. User reruns the check
4. If the fix worked, the highlight disappears
5. Repeat until the report is clean

This is a key workflow to communicate: **DocAuditor is not a one-and-done scan. It's an iterative loop — check, fix, recheck — until the work is ready.**

## 10. Credit System — Deep Dive

The credit system is central to DocAuditor's pricing. Marketing must understand it thoroughly.

### How Credits Work

1. Users purchase or receive credits
2. When a check is run, credits are deducted based on: **word count × credit rate for the check type(s) selected**
3. Word counts are rounded up to the nearest 1,000 (minimum 1,000 words)
4. Different check types consume credits at different rates

### Credit Rates Per Check Type

| Check Combination      | Credits per Word | Credits for 1,000 Words | Credits for 5,000 Words |
| ---------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| AI Originality only    | 0.20             | 200                     | 1,000                   |
| Plagiarism Source only | 0.35             | 350                     | 1,750                   |
| Alignment Brief only   | 0.30             | 300                     | 1,500                   |
| AI + Plagiarism        | 0.50             | 500                     | 2,500                   |
| AI + Alignment         | 0.50             | 500                     | 2,500                   |
| Plagiarism + Alignment | 0.65             | 650                     | 3,250                   |
| All Three (Full Check) | 0.75             | 750                     | 3,750                   |

### Credit Value

- 1 credit = approximately $0.0007
- A full check on a 1,000-word document costs 750 credits ≈ $0.53 (pay-per-check) or ≈ $0.18 (subscription)

### Important Rules

- **Minimum word count:** 1,000 words (a 500-word document is charged as 1,000 words)
- **Rounding:** Word counts are always rounded UP to the nearest 1,000
- **No expiry:** Purchased credits never expire
- **Subscription credits:** 40,000 credits refresh monthly with the subscription. Unused monthly credits do not roll over (purchased top-up credits do not expire)

## 16. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Product Questions

**Q: What is AlphaDrafts DocAuditor?**
A: DocAuditor is a pre-submission document verification platform. It checks your written work for AI-generated content flags, plagiarism/source matches, and missing requirements — then shows you exactly what to fix, sentence by sentence.

**Q: What does the Alignment Brief Check do?**
A: It compares your document against the original instructions, rubric, or project brief. You get a checklist of every requirement — showing which ones are met and which ones are missing — so you can fix gaps before submission.

**Q: How do the AI Originality and Plagiarism Source Checks work?**
A: The AI Originality Check scans each sentence for patterns common in AI-generated text. The Plagiarism Source Check compares your content against published sources and shows you direct links to matches. Both provide sentence-level detail.

**Q: Does DocAuditor change or rewrite my content?**
A: No. DocAuditor flags issues and tells you what to look at. You decide what to change. Your words stay yours.

## 18. What DocAuditor Cannot Do (Limitations)

This is equally important for marketing. Never overstate the product. Here are the hard boundaries:

1. **DocAuditor does NOT rewrite or generate content.** It flags problems. The user fixes them. This is by design — we are a verification tool, not a writing tool.

2. **DocAuditor does NOT guarantee 100% accuracy.** AI detection, plagiarism detection, and requirement extraction are probabilistic. Results may be incomplete or may contain false positives/negatives. Users must review results and apply their own judgment.

3. **DocAuditor does NOT replace human judgment.** It is a supplementary tool. It does not replace peer review, editorial review, or institutional academic integrity processes.

4. **DocAuditor does NOT guarantee compliance** with any institution's policies, journal guidelines, or legal requirements. It helps users identify potential issues — compliance responsibility remains with the user.

5. **DocAuditor does NOT check grammar, spelling, or writing quality.** It is not a proofreading or writing improvement tool. It checks for AI flags, source matches, and requirement alignment only.

6. **DocAuditor does NOT work offline.** It is a web-based platform requiring an internet connection.

7. **DocAuditor does NOT support real-time collaboration.** It is currently a single-user experience per document.

8. **DocAuditor does NOT provide legal, medical, or professional advice.** Results should not be used for high-risk decisions without appropriate human review by qualified professionals.

9. **The AI Originality Check does NOT definitively prove content was or wasn't AI-generated.** It flags patterns consistent with AI writing. There is no tool that can make this determination with 100% certainty.

10. **The Plagiarism Source Check may NOT find every match.** Coverage depends on the source index available. Some sources (paywalled, unpublished, or very recent content) may not be indexed.

### Marketing Disclaimer Language

Always include a variation of this in marketing materials that discuss accuracy:

> "DocAuditor verification results may be incomplete or inaccurate. Always review results before relying on them. DocAuditor is a verification aid, not a guarantee."
